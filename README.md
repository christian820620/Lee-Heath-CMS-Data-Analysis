## Power BI Report

The final Power BI report was designed as an interactive hospital benchmarking tool that allowed Lee Health to explore performance across multiple areas of hospital quality, outcomes, patient experience, and operations.

Rather than focusing on a single metric, the report brought together multiple CMS and public healthcare datasets into one reporting solution backed by our PostgreSQL database and SQL analysis.

The Power BI report included analysis of:

* **Top 20 U.S. Hospitals** — comparison of nationally recognized hospitals used as performance benchmarks
* **Performance Compared to National Average** — identifies measures where hospital performance is above, below, or similar to national benchmarks
* **Below National Average Measures** — highlights specific areas where performance may need improvement
* **Deaths & Complications** — analysis of hospital outcomes across CMS mortality and complication measures
* **Unplanned Hospital Visits** — analysis of readmissions and other unplanned return visits
* **Specialty Scores** — comparison of hospital performance across different medical specialties
* **Facility General Information** — hospital characteristics, CMS ratings, ownership information, emergency services, and other facility-level information
* **Patient Experience** — analysis of patient experience and HCAHPS-related performance
* **Healthcare-Associated Infections** — comparison of hospital infection measures against national benchmarks
* **Hospital Benchmarking** — comparison of Lee Health with Florida hospitals and nationally recognized hospitals
* **Additional Performance Metrics** — supporting views and filters for exploring hospital performance across the datasets included in the project
